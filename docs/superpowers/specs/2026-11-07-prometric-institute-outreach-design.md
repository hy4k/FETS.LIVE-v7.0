# Prometric institute outreach inside Actionables

The user wants large client boxes inside Actionables, with the existing centre-development workspace under Pearson VUE and a new Prometric workspace for CMA institute relationships and mock-exam candidate intake. The primary goal is institute batches attending FETS centres; institute-hosted platform support is secondary. The attached draft guides a transparent, consultative research-first conversation, with complete structured follow-up rather than isolated notes. User confirmed one owner per institute and shared authorised-manager oversight across both centres.

## Experience and records

- Actionables opens on two large buttons: Pearson VUE (existing workflow, unchanged records) and Prometric (CMA institute partnerships). Back navigation and a client query parameter make the workspace easy to revisit. The existing News alias retains its old workspace.
- Prometric opens with an institute list, overdue/today/upcoming/needs-next-step queues, owner/centre/stage/search filters and clear candidate totals. Staff see their owned institutes; authorised managers/SuperAdmin see all and can reassign ownership. All access is checked from canonical active/verified staff, Actionables membership and User Management permissions, never editable JWT user metadata.
- Institute profile: name, city/state, business contact person/role, business phone/email, website, preferred FETS centre, owner, relationship stage, contact permission and notes. No student name/phone/database collection. Duplicate normalised name+city is rejected; phone/email matches warn before staff deliberately add a distinct organisation.
- Guided call logging includes the script beside the form; contact/outcome/time/channel, structured candidate-experience questions and concern tags, current mock practice, appetite for FETS-centre mocks/platform, advisory-group interest, short summary, permission for follow-up, and an explicit dated next action or closure reason. The history is append-only. Corrections are additional notes/calls, not silent edits to past conversations.
- One open next step per institute avoids duplicate reminders. Saving a call atomically appends history, supersedes the earlier open step without pretending it was completed, then creates the agreed next action or records closure. Follow-ups can be completed/rescheduled with a note and version check. An active institute without an open next step is flagged, never silently lost.
- Do-not-contact is explicit, keeps history and cancels open outbound work. It suppresses normal outbound logging/follow-ups. A manager may restore permission only with a recorded explanation; an inbound permission update can be recorded by the owner. No automatic calls, emails, WhatsApp messages or external publication are authorised.
- Candidate pipeline uses distinct mock batches/opportunities, not accumulated call totals. Each batch records name, delivery FETS-centre vs institute-platform, centre, planned date, status potential/confirmed/completed/cancelled, estimated count, confirmed count and attended count. Counts are whole/nonnegative; attended cannot exceed confirmed and only completed batches have attendance. Potential batches cannot claim confirmed/attended seats; cancelled batches are excluded from active totals. Show incoming and platform totals separately. These are staff-entered partnership counts, not candidate registrations. No automatic Calendar/candidate mutations; link to Calendar for capacity review.
- Useful stages: new, contacted, understanding needs, interested, planning, active partner, paused, closed. Closed/paused outcomes need a reason. A successful call does not automatically promise a mock date, price or provider endorsement.

## Call guide

Write concise English scripts in the user's draft style, with a 3–4 minute core conversation, optional deeper questions, receptionist/busy/sceptical/no-interest branches, recap and follow-up permission. Be clear that FETS is gathering feedback and exploring optional mock support; avoid disguising a sales pitch as independent or provider-sponsored research. Do not assert authorisation, partner sponsorship, fees, guaranteed capacity or platform capabilities beyond confirmed FETS arrangements. Do not request student contact lists, recalled exam questions or confidential exam content. Offer FETS-centre mock discussion first and institute-hosted platform as an alternative; neither is mandatory. Script remains visible even before institutes exist.

## Implementation and boundaries

Use current React/TypeScript/Supabase; preserve the original deployment checkout. Add private RLS institutes/calls/followups/batches and checked versioned public invoker RPCs. Existing Pearson/mission tables, business bookings and candidate/roster data are untouched. Actor/owner checks and scope locks prevent stale/reassigned staff writes. No destructive delete workflow: close records and preserve history. Complete server-side context reads and identity-scoped client cache avoid truncated or cross-user data. Use IST dates/times, including overdue calculations independent of the host timezone. Currency/prices and actual mock-test delivery are not invented or implemented by this CRM task.

The current feature request authorises implementation and the owner/oversight choice is confirmed. The builder chooses UI organisation and safe validation limits, records them in the plan, and prepares a testable result. No outreach messages are sent by the agent.
