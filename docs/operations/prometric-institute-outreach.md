# Prometric institute partnerships

Open **Actionables → Prometric**. Pearson VUE continues to open the existing institutional mission workspace.

## Start an institute relationship

1. Open **Call guide** before calling. Use the opening, listen to the institute's experience, and offer a centre mock only where useful. The institute-platform discussion is an alternative.
2. Select **Add institute** and enter its business contact, city and preferred centre. One staff member owns the relationship; an authorised manager can select or change that owner.
3. Check any shared phone/email warning before creating a distinct institute. The same normalised institute name and city cannot be created twice.
4. Open the institute and **Log conversation**. Record the outcome, relevant research answers, concerns, service interest, and follow-up permission separately.
5. Set one dated next step, or record why no next step is needed. Times are shown in IST. A new conversation supersedes an older open task without marking it completed.

## Work the queue

Use **Overdue**, **Today**, **Upcoming**, or **Needs next step**. Search by institute, location or business contact; filter by owner, centre and stage. Managers see the shared pipeline across both centres, while staff see their assigned institutes.

Inside an institute:

- **Next step** lets its owner or a manager reschedule, complete or cancel the current task.
- **Log conversation** records a new call, meeting, correspondence, inbound contact or internal note.
- **Mock batches** keeps distinct cohorts and their progress.
- **History** preserves recorded conversations and loads older entries in pages.
- **Profile** updates business details; managers can reassign ownership with a reason.

Completing the last task without a replacement places an active institute in **Needs next step**. Paused, closed and do-not-contact relationships are excluded from that chasing queue.

## Count candidate opportunities

Create a distinct batch per cohort, then update that batch as it progresses:

- **Potential estimate**: expected candidate count for a potential cohort.
- **Confirmed ahead**: confirmed count on a confirmed cohort.
- **Attended**: actual attendance on a completed cohort, bounded by its confirmed count.

Incoming FETS-centre cohorts and institute-platform cohorts have separate ledgers. These stages are separate measures and must not be added together as unique students. Repeated calls do not increase candidate counts. Cancelled cohorts do not contribute; historical attendance remains visible when an institute relationship closes.

Use **Review Calendar capacity** before agreeing dates or numbers. Recording a cohort does not reserve seats, register candidates, create a Calendar session, or provide the actual exam-delivery platform.

## Permissions and record care

Owners must already have verified, active Actionables access. Canonical User Management permissions and existing Actionables membership govern access; managers can oversee records and SuperAdmin has full authorised access. Creating an institute does not grant another user new application permissions.

Calls are append-only. Correct or clarify an earlier conversation with a new entry. Institute ownership/permission changes retain an audit record. Concurrent edits require a reload; an unfinished draft remains visible until explicitly reloaded.

**Do not contact** cancels outbound follow-up work and blocks new outbound interactions. Internal notes and internal reviews remain available. An owner can restore permission after documented inbound permission; a manager can restore it with a reason.

The guide identifies this as a FETS initiative. Do not imply CMA, IMA or Prometric sponsorship, promise unverified prices or availability, collect student contact lists, or request confidential exam content. This workspace records work; it does not send calls, emails or WhatsApp messages.
