# Proposed Play Store listing — review before publication

**App title:** FETS LIVE

**Short description:** Your centre, connected. Plan shifts, track sessions and get work done.

**Full description:**

Bring your working day together with FETS LIVE, the staff workspace for FETS
centres. Keep your team, schedules and daily responsibilities close at hand.

• See your centre's sessions and operational overview.
• Plan the calendar, manage candidate registrations and track arrivals.
• View rosters, shifts and daily duties, with leave and overtime tools.
• Organise your personal workspace, checklists and handovers in My Desk.
• Keep conversations and follow-through together in Team Space and Actionables.
• Find the tools you need with a searchable workspace menu.
• Access FETS AI and administrative tools where your account has permission.

The refreshed mobile workspace features clear navigation, larger touch controls
and a new FETS LIVE identity. Features and records depend on your staff account,
centre access and enabled services. An authorised FETS staff account and an
internet connection are required. This app is intended for FETS operations.

**What's new (proposed):**

Meet the refreshed FETS LIVE mobile workspace. Navigate daily work with Home,
Calendar, Roster and My Desk. Find Team Space, Actionables, The Shift and your
other tools from More. Enjoy refreshed branding, phone-friendly layouts,
clearer controls and Android back navigation.
